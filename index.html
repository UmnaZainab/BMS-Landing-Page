About Us

Built around the way a company actually operates.

NExBMS is a centralized Business Management System by Nexum Global Solutions. It brings verified people, delivery, finance and workplace workflows into focused role-based views without exposing unnecessary controls.

Clear responsibility

Employee, Manager, HR and administrative workspaces support the actions appropriate to each role.

Connected workflows

Attendance, leave, payroll, projects, tasks, assets and communication remain visible in one system.

Protected operations

JWT authentication, server-enforced permissions and audit logging support controlled access.

Verified Features

The operating layer for everyday company work.

People & Access

Employee profiles, activation controls, profile pictures and role-based access.

Attendance

Check-in, check-out, working hours, late tracking and attendance calendar.

Leave Management

Balances, requests, history and department-aware approval workflows.

Payroll

Salary summaries, deductions, CSV export and downloadable PDF payslips.

Projects & Tasks

Project creation, task assignment, due dates, progress and status tracking.

Assets & Requests

Communications

Administration

Recruitment & Onboarding
