About the Platform

Built around the way a real company operates.

NExBMS is a centralized Business Management System by Nexum Global Solutions. It unifies HR, Attendance, Recruitment, Payroll and Project operations in a single role-aware workspace, so every department moves forward on the same data.

Core Modules

The operating layer for everyday company work.

Six tightly integrated modules cover the operational backbone of a modern business, from department shifts to payroll payslips and the company notice board.

Department Attendance

Shift-aware check-ins for employees with late tracking, working-hour calculation and a live attendance calendar.

Recruitment & Auto Interview Emails

Applicant tracking, scheduled interviews and automated email invites keep hiring moving without manual follow-up.

Automated Payroll & Payslips

Gross to net salary, tax and benefit deductions and one click PDF payslips, generated by the system every cycle.

Leave Management

Balances, requests and department-aware approval workflows, with history and remaining days visible to employees and HR.

IT Hardware Tracking

Full-lifecycle asset management, including issue approvals, custodian assignments, maintenance notes and return clearance in one trail.

Notice Board

Targeted company-wide and department-specific announcements with personal notifications and read-receipt history.
